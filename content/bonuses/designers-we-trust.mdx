## Working with a designer

- **Look through their past work first** and point to the pieces closest to what you want.
- **Write a short brief:** what it's for, where it will be used (tee, hoodie, logo, packaging), colors, and two or three references.
- **Agree on the price, number of revisions and file formats** before they start. Ask for vector files for anything that will be printed.
- **Confirm you own the final artwork** and can use it on products you sell.
- Mention Inertia when you reach out.
