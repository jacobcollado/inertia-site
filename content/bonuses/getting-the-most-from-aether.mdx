## Start here

Aether was built by design engineers, not assembled from a template. It has been in active development for four years, shaped by how real shoppers move through real stores, and it keeps improving. Every update is yours for life.

That history is good news for you. The parts that decide whether someone buys, finding a product, choosing an option, getting through the cart and checkout, already work. They were built from data, not guesswork, so you don't need to touch them on day one. A store running Aether with its defaults is already a store with no friction between browsing and buying.

What Aether can't do on its own is supply your brand. The difference between a good Aether store and a great one comes down to three things:

1. **Following the setup in our docs**, so the theme looks like you and not like a demo.
2. **Strong imagery** that makes people stop scrolling. See *Imagery that converts*.
3. **Clean product shots** that make every product page look deliberate. See *Shooting your products*.

Get those three right and the theme does the rest.

## Your first hour

Work through these in order. Each links to the full walkthrough in the docs.

1. **Make it yours.** Set your colors, upload your logo and pick a type size. [First-time setup](/docs?from=aether#aether-first-setup)
2. **Choose your hero.** Full width if you have one great lifestyle image, split if you want to lead with a product, text only for a launch. The hero is the single biggest visual decision on your store, so give it your best image. [Hero](/docs?from=aether#aether-hero)
3. **Set up your menu.** Nest links under a top-level item and it opens as a mega menu. Put a featured image or collection on the right side to give it a reason to be opened. [Header](/docs?from=aether#aether-header)
4. **Check the product page.** Add up to four trust badges under the add to cart button, and rename the tabs to match what your customers ask about. [Product page](/docs?from=aether#aether-product-page)
5. **Turn on filters.** Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app and filters appear on your collection pages on their own. [Collection page](/docs?from=aether#aether-collection)
6. **Pick one way to capture emails.** The signup card, the popup and the SMS tab can all run at once, but one or two is usually right. Open your store as a first-time visitor and check nothing stacks on top of anything else. [Email and SMS capture](/docs?from=aether#aether-email-capture)

## What to leave alone

It's tempting to rearrange everything. Resist it in these places:

- **The product page structure.** Images on the left, details on the right that stay in view, sticky add to cart once the main button scrolls away. That layout is there because it converts.
- **The cart drawer.** The free shipping bar and the layout around it are tuned to get one more item into the cart. Change the threshold to match your shipping, not the design.
- **Checkout.** It's Shopify's, and it's fast. Adding apps that inject steps before it only adds friction.

If something feels like it needs changing here, ask us first. There's usually a setting for it, and if there isn't, that's feedback we want.

## Small things that add up

- **Use the announcement bar for one message at a time.** Free shipping, a launch, a restock. Rotating through five messages means nobody reads any of them. [Announcement bar](/docs?from=aether#aether-announcement-bar)
- **Turn on color swatches** if your products come in colors. Hovering a swatch loads that color's image, which saves a click for every shopper who cares about color.
- **Let dark mode be.** Aether follows each visitor's device setting and both modes are designed separately. If your imagery is dark and moody, you can offer a switch in Theme settings.
- **Use your own fonts** if your brand has them. No code needed. [Custom fonts](/docs?from=aether#aether-custom-fonts)

## Staying current

Aether keeps getting better, and every update is included. Before you update, take a backup, and if you've edited any theme code, read how to keep those edits. [Updates](/docs?from=aether#aether-how-to-update)

Stuck on anything? Reply to any email from us. You'll hear back from the people who built the theme.
