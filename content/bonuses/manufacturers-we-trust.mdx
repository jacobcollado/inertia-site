## Before you order

- **Always order a sample first**, even when you're in a hurry. It's the only way to check fit, fabric and finish before committing to a bulk run.
- **Send references, not just descriptions.** Photos of garments you like, measurements, fabric swatches if you have them.
- **Agree on everything in writing:** quantity, price per unit, timeline, and what happens if a batch has defects.
- **Factor in shipping and customs** on top of production time. A 4 to 6 week bulk order is not a 4 to 6 week delivery.
